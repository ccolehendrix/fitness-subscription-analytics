# fitness-subscription-analytics
Sprint 7 project


Customer Cohort Analysis: After how many months do customers typically cancel their subscriptions?

Using the Customer Cohort Chart: Based on the data that was analyzed and presented, I would conclude that most customers end up canceling their subscriptions after one month (ex. The drop-off from January to February.)

Revenue Forecast: What is the expected subscription revenue over the next 12 months? Does the revenue forecast show evidence of seasonality?

Using the Revenue Forecast Chart: Based on the subscription forecast there is no appearance of a seasonal response, however the response was observed in some of the other classifications.

CAC vs. LTV: How long does it take for the 2024 and 2025 cohorts to recover their customer acquisition cost?

Using the CAC vs. LTV Chart: Based on the data from 2024 and 2025 cohorts, the month where revenue was starting to be converted into profit was July in both 2024 and 2025.
