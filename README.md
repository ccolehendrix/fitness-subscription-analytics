Sprint 7 project


Customer Cohort Analysis: After how many months do customers typically cancel their subscriptions?

Using the Customer Cohort Chart: Based on the data that was analyzed and presented, I would conclude that most customers end up canceling their subscriptions after 2 months of use.  The dramatic drop-off during the first 2 months of use demonstrates this (100% of users leads to around 85% of users leads to around 78% of users and that leads us to around 50% of users. The next 21 months have around 20% loss of customers vs. the first two months of service has around 50% loss of customers.

Revenue Forecast: What is the expected subscription revenue over the next 12 months? Does the revenue forecast show evidence of seasonality?

Using the Revenue Forecast Chart: Based on the subscription forecast the projected revenue over the next 12 months is around $267,483.  There also appears to have no seasonal response for the subscription category of sales.

CAC vs. LTV: How long does it take for the 2024 and 2025 cohorts to recover their customer acquisition cost?

Using the CAC vs. LTV Chart: Based on the data from 2024 and 2025 cohorts, the month where revenue was starting to be converted into profit was around December in both 2024 and 2025.
